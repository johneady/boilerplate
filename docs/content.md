# Pages, blog and contact form

Everything visitors read on the site is managed under **Content** in the
admin panel. Administrators, managers and editors can work on it.

## Pages

**Content → Pages** holds the site's pages. Four are created when you
install: **About**, **Contact**, **Privacy Policy** and **Terms &
Conditions**. Each appears at its own address, such as `/about`.

- **Rewrite the legal pages before going live.** The privacy policy and terms
  you are given are placeholders, not legal advice.
- A page has a title, an address (the "slug"), its text, and an optional image.
  The **SEO description** is the summary search engines and link previews
  show.
- A page that is not published is hidden from visitors. A published page can
  also be linked from the site's footer.
- Some addresses are reserved for the site itself (for example `blog`,
  `login` and `health`), so a page cannot use them.

## The blog

The blog is **off** when you install. Turn it on under **Admin → Settings →
Blog**. It then appears at `/blog`, and **Posts**, **Categories** and **Tags**
appear under Content.

- A post's **publish date** decides what visitors see. With no date, the post
  is a draft. A date in the past means it is published. A date in the future
  schedules it, and it appears on that day without you doing anything.
- Each post has one category and any number of tags. Visitors can browse by
  either, and follow the blog in a feed reader at `/blog/feed`.
- A cover image is optional, and is shown on the blog list and at the top of
  the post.
- Editors can manage their own posts. Administrators and managers can manage
  everyone's.

Turning the blog off hides it from visitors and from the admin panel, but
keeps every post. They come back unchanged when you turn it on again.

## The contact form

The **Contact** page carries a form. Every message is saved under **Content →
Contact submissions**, where you can mark it as handled once you have replied.

To have each message emailed to you as well, enter a contact address under
**Admin → Settings → Business**. That address is also shown in the site's
footer. With it left blank, messages are saved but not emailed.

The form has spam protection built in, so you don't need to add a captcha.

## Images and files

Everything uploaded, such as page images, post covers, the logo and profile
pictures, is listed under **System → Media library**.

Uploaded photos are resized for the web, and their hidden photo data (such as
the location and camera details stored in a phone picture) is removed before
visitors can see them. A new image can take a moment to appear while this
happens.

## Your logo and site name

Under **Admin → Settings → Brand**, upload your logo. It is used across the
site, in the browser tab and in link previews on social media. The same tab
holds the title and description search engines show. Your business name,
address and phone number are under the **Business** tab, and appear in the
site's footer.
